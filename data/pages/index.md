### MoveMemorE vehicle motion replay 
_For my Bachelor's capstone project, my team developed VR software that uses GPS and inertial data to replay recorded journeys. The software connects directly to the [NOVA motion platform](https://www.eight360.com/) to control its 360° motion, synchronising physical movement with the VR visuals to recreate the sensation of the original journey._

![Our MoveMemorE simulator running on the NOVA motion platform](media/nova.png)
